# External validation consent and privacy notice

## Purpose

This pilot studies whether a repository-level completion evidence report changes the speed, consistency, or confidence of software-review decisions compared with an agent summary alone.

## What participants do

Participants review ten prepared coding-task cases, make acceptance decisions, identify blocking issues, and answer short confidence, ambiguity, and maintenance-willingness questions. Expected duration is 30–60 minutes.

## Data collected

- pseudonymous participant ID;
- software-review experience band and relevant task-family familiarity;
- condition/order assignment;
- timing and decision fields defined in `data-template.csv`;
- optional free-text explanation;
- consent timestamp and protocol version.

The study does not request source-code credentials, employer-confidential code, private repository access, government identifiers, precise address, financial information, or health information.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. A participant may stop at any time before submitting the completed session. They may decline free-text questions. No employment, academic, or service benefit depends on participation.

## Risks

Risks are limited but include time cost, frustration, and accidental disclosure in free text. Participants are instructed not to paste private source code, credentials, client names, or employer-confidential details.

## Storage and separation

Use a random participant ID in the analysis dataset. Store any contact/identity key separately with restricted access. Do not commit the identity key to this public repository. Before publishing raw comments, remove accidental names, repository identifiers, credentials, or other personal data.

## Publication

Aggregate results, anonymized records, analysis code, exclusions, and limitations may be published. Small-cell reporting must avoid combinations that could identify an individual. Direct quotes require a separate explicit opt-in and redaction review.

## Retention and withdrawal

The study operator must state a retention period before recruitment. A participant can request deletion by participant ID until the anonymized dataset is frozen for publication. After irreversible aggregation, individual removal may no longer be possible.

## Consent record

Before starting, the participant confirms:

1. they read this notice;
2. they are participating voluntarily;
3. they understand that this is a software-review study, not employment evaluation;
4. they will not submit secrets or confidential code;
5. they consent to anonymized analysis under the stated protocol.
